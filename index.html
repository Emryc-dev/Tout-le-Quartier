Shipping Policy

Thank you for shopping with us! We’re committed to delivering your order as quickly and efficiently as possible. Please review our shipping timelines and details below:

Dispatch Overview

Destination Processing Shipping
United States 1–3 business days 3–5 business days
Canada 3–4 business days 3–7 business days
International (Pre-Order) 3–4 business days* 1–3 weeks

*After the pre-order processing window closes. All timelines are business-day estimates.

Tracking & Delivery

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email. Please allow up to 48 hours for the tracking information to update.

  1. Step 1 Order processed within your region’s dispatch window
  2. Step 2 Shipment confirmation and tracking number sent via email
  3. Step 3 Carrier scan and live tracking update within 48 hours
05

Important Notes

  • Shipping times are estimates and may be affected by carrier delays, holidays, weather conditions, or customs clearance.
  • For time-sensitive orders or special shipping requests, please contact us before placing your order.
